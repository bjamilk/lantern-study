# Lantern Study 1.0.33

Know where you stand, from day one.

- **Exam readiness on your Dashboard**: every course you're enrolled in gets an
  honest readiness bar — how much of the course outline you've started and how
  well it's going — with a "Start here" pointer so a brand-new student knows
  exactly where to begin, and an exam countdown once a date is set.
- Tap a course for the topic-by-topic breakdown, and see what your classmates
  find hardest (shown only once 20+ students on the course have study data).
- Readiness updates as you study: tests and flashcard reviews both move it.
- The marketplace is in a **private pilot** for now — a clear note explains
  this instead of an error. Everything else in Lantern stays open.
- Fixed: the academic-profile setup sheet no longer hides its Continue /
  Skip buttons under the Android system bars.
