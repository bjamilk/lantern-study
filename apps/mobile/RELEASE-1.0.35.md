# Lantern Study 1.0.35

Communities you can actually talk in.

- Every community now has a **Community chat** — a shared lounge that opens
  with one tap from the Discover card or the community page. No setting up a
  group first: if you belong to the community, you can talk in it.
- **Your communities** in Discover now shows the communities you actually
  belong to, with everything else under "More to join". A brand-new account no
  longer sees another university's communities presented as its own.
- The Discover tab is called **Community** again.
- Study rooms are truly temporary now: a room closes on its own after 6 hours
  (or when the last person leaves) and is deleted a few days later.
