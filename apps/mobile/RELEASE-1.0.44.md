# Lantern Study 1.0.44

Community posts work like Twitter, and shared photos stop disappearing.

- **Four actions on every post.** Favorite, Repost, Comment, Bookmark and
  Share replace the single emoji reaction. Tap the heart to favorite; tap
  again to undo. Counts stay hidden until there is something to count.
- **Repost.** Put a good post back at the top of the board so people who
  missed it see it, credited to whoever wrote it. Add your own words first if
  you want to. One repost per person per post.
- **Bookmark.** "Save for me" is now a real account-level list. Your saves
  follow you to a new phone and to the web instead of dying with the app, and
  **Saved posts** shows them all in one place. Anything you had saved on this
  phone is imported the first time you open it. Nobody can see what you saved.
- **Share.** Send a post anywhere with the normal share sheet. The link only
  opens for people who are already in that community — everyone else sees an
  invitation to join, never the post.
- **Photos and GIFs in a post.** Write a title, write your post and attach a
  photo or an animated GIF, all as one post instead of two. GIFs stay
  animated. Photos appear in the list; on low-data mode they stay behind a tap,
  as before.
- **Fixed: shared photos vanished after a day.** Every photo posted in a chat
  or on a board became invisible 24 hours later — no error, no placeholder, the
  picture simply gone, and a broken image on the web. Photos now load however
  old they are, including ones already posted.
- **Fixed: your own new post showed you as "Member".** A post you had just
  written showed the author as "Member" with grey initials instead of your name
  and photo, until you closed and reopened the app. Your name is now correct the
  moment the post appears.
- **Fixed: reaction counts read zero.** Counts on messages and posts showed
  nothing until someone reacted while you were watching. They are now correct
  the moment a conversation opens.
