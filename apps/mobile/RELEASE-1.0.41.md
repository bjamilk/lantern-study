# Lantern Study 1.0.41

Communities became places you stay in, the Shop got its screen back, and the
5% marketplace fee moved inside the price.

- **A community is now a server.** Open one and you get its `# lounge`, its
  channels, its open study rooms and its members — all on one screen. Opening a
  channel keeps you inside the community, and Back returns to it.
- **Members, with who's here.** Online and Offline sections with status dots,
  plus Owner, Admin and Moderator badges. The header says how many members and
  how many are online right now.
- **Channels are the study groups you already have.** Messages, unread badges,
  mentions and everything else work exactly as before. Create a new channel with
  the + beside TEXT CHANNELS; it is filed in the community automatically.
- **Rooms replaced Market in the community tabs.** A room stays open for 24
  hours (it was 6), then closes and disappears. Start one with the + beside
  STUDY ROOMS, or from the Room tab.
- **You pay the listed price.** The 5% marketplace fee is now inside the price
  instead of added at checkout. Buyers pay exactly what the listing says;
  sellers receive 95%, itemised on the Payouts screen.
- **Shop search folds away.** The Shop header shows a search icon; tap it to
  search. Alerts, Cart, You and Sell now share the row. Recent searches appear
  in the search suggestions instead of a row of their own.
- **Community and Jobs in your profile menu**, in the order Community, Jobs,
  Low-data mode, Dark mode, Settings.
