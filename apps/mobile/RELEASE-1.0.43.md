# Lantern Study 1.0.43

Communities get boards, and you can put your own photo behind a chat.

- **A community is no longer a chat app.** Open one and you get **General** —
  its one live conversation — plus **Boards**, **Study groups**, **Study rooms**
  and **Members**, all on one screen.
- **Boards.** A board is a list of posts, newest first, each with a title, a
  reaction and its own comments, and one pinned post everyone sees. It is for
  the things worth reading a week later: past questions, the exam timetable,
  "who has the handout".
- **Study belongs to study groups.** Questions, tests, games and challenges are
  no longer inside a community channel. Start a study group from the community
  and it opens in Chat with the full toolkit, while staying listed in the
  community so members can find and join it.
- **General keeps talking.** The community's own chat is still a chat — just
  without the study apparatus.
- **Your own photo behind a chat.** Chat menu → Chat background → choose a
  photo, for that conversation; or set a default for every chat in Settings. A
  single chat can still be set plain. The photo stays on your phone, costs no
  data, and text stays readable over it.
- **Fixed.** A new board post no longer shows its author as "Member" until you
  restart the app, and the floating AI credit badge no longer covers the +
  buttons on a community screen.
