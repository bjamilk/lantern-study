# Lantern Study 1.0.32

Marketplace ratings, everywhere you browse.

- Stars with review counts on marketplace cards and the Related-on-campus rail.
- New "Top rated" sort and a minimum-rating filter (★4/3/2/1 & up) in the
  marketplace filters.
- Every listing now has a ratings summary: average, star histogram you can tap
  to read one star bucket, and review sorting (Top, Most recent, Highest,
  Lowest).
- "Verified purchase" badges on reviews from buyers who completed a purchase.
- Mark reviews as Helpful; the most helpful reviews rise to the top.

On the web app, Chats now opens as its own side column next to the navigation
instead of collapsing inside it.
