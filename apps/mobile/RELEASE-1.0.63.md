# Lantern Study 1.0.63

Recording a lecture is now something you can trust in a real classroom, and a
new set starts with your syllabus instead of an empty page.

- **Check your sound before the lecture starts.** The recorder shows a live
  level bar, tells you whether the audio is good ("too quiet — move closer",
  "clipping — move back") and whether the connection is, and lets you set the
  language you are speaking so the transcript comes out in it. On the phone the
  level shows once the take is running; the rest is there before you start.
- **The transcript grows while the class is still going.** A lecture is recorded
  in five-minute pieces that upload as each one closes and are transcribed on
  arrival, so timestamped cards appear as you go. Close the app at minute seven
  and everything already uploaded is safe — reopen and choose **Carry on** or
  **Finish**. Each piece can be played back or downloaded from the new **Audio
  files** tab. It costs exactly what it did before: one AI use per 15 minutes,
  counted across the whole lecture, not per piece.
- **Enhanced notes that know what you know.** Tell the enhancer how much of the
  subject you already have (or tap Basic / Advanced) and attach a material from
  the set for it to read alongside the transcript.
- **Sync with your class.** A new or empty set opens on one card: upload the
  syllabus (PDF or Word) and Lantern reads out the weeks and any exam dates, or
  add an exam date by hand. The set counts down to the exam, and the Home
  screen's **Upcoming exam** card — which had never had anything under it —
  now shows a real date. One AI use per syllabus; nothing charged if it fails.
- **Imports tell you what is happening.** Bring in a file and you see three
  stages that follow what is really going on — uploaded, processed, flashcards
  and quiz made — with a Retry on the stage that failed, then a choice of where
  to go next. A **Creation progress** button in the set header lists recent
  imports and generations. Plain-text (.txt, .md) files and iPhone HEIC photos
  are accepted now.

**Under the floor:** a security fix to the library that reads Anki `.apkg`
archives (updated, and a size cap added before anything is unpacked), and the
set page's recent-uploads list no longer shows another student's titles on a
shared browser.

**Not yet:** the study plan does not build units from syllabus weeks (a plan
whose topics open nothing would be a dead door), and the phone remembers "Skip
for now" on the device rather than the account.
