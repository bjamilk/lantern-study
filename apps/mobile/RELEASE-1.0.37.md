# Lantern Study 1.0.37

Chat fixes, and semester on your profile.

- **Selected messages now highlight.** Long-press a message and the whole row
  is tinted while the action bar is open, so it is always clear which message
  you are about to reply to, star, pin or forward.
- **Starred messages are findable.** The chat menu has a new
  **Starred messages** entry that filters the conversation to just your starred
  ones — tap "Show all" to go back. Starring used to be a one-way trip.
- **Pin is no longer blocked.** The floating sync chip sat on top of the
  pinned-message bar and swallowed taps meant for it. It now lets taps through
  and hides itself entirely when you are online and everything is synced.
- **Cleaner chat list.** The lines between chats and groups are gone.
- **First / Second semester** can now be set on your academic profile, in
  Settings → Academic and during profile setup.
