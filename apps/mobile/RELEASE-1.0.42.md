# Lantern Study 1.0.42

A freeze fix, and plainer cards.

- **Fixed: the app froze when you opened a community chat from the Chat
  screen.** Any chat created inside a community locked the app on the first
  tap, and again every time you went back to it, until you force-closed it.
  It now opens normally.
- **Cards are plain.** The coloured bar and tinted fill are gone from the
  dashboard greeting, the study cards, saved sessions, offline storage and
  notification rows. Colour on an edge now only appears where it means
  something: a quoted reply, a warning, and the chat you have open.
