# Lantern Study 1.0.40

The Shop now works the way you expect a shop to: your cart, your orders, what
you're selling and what needs your attention are all one tap away.

- **Cart and You, on every Shop screen.** The cart shows how many items are in
  it; the You icon shows how many things are waiting on you.
- **A You hub.** Switch between Buying and Selling (the app remembers which).
  Buying: Your Orders, Buy Again, Cart, Saved, Offers you made, Messages to
  sellers. Selling: orders to hand over, offers received, buyer questions,
  your listings, customers, study products, your shop, semester packs — and
  **Payouts**, which tells you plainly if you haven't set up a bank account yet.
- **Payouts.** Your bank details and an earnings ledger: each sale with its
  status — awaiting payment, paid, payout pending, paid out — and when it was
  paid out.
- **Your Listings is the seller's home.** A "Needs you" strip at the top shows
  orders to hand over, offers awaiting your answer and unread buyer questions,
  or says you're all caught up. Every seller tool is a chip, not a hidden menu.
- **Orders that read like Amazon's.** Status pills (Hand over, Paid, Completed,
  Cancelled…), the rows that need you first, pull to refresh, and a Buy Again
  view.
- **Quick access on the Shop home:** Your Orders, Cart, Saved and Selling cards
  under the departments.
- **Small things that add up.** Cart rows show the item's picture; an empty cart
  offers Continue shopping and your Saved items; Saved items can go straight
  to the cart; after adding to cart you can jump to it; the listing form clears
  after you publish; badges only count what is actually your turn.
- **Navigation.** Shop lives in the top bar; Jobs lives in your profile menu
  beside Settings; the Dashboard tab is called Dashboard.

**Fixes**

- Tapping an order update, a new buyer question, or an offer notification now
  opens the right screen. All three were dead or misrouted.
- Publishing a study pack from a deck of 45+ cards failed with no message.
- "Orders" from any seller screen opened purchase history instead of sales.
- The marketplace could report a dropped connection as "not on the private
  pilot" and stay that way; it now retries and says what actually happened.
