# Lantern Study 1.0.39

The marketplace becomes a real shop: Shop and Jobs each get their own tab, and
products are filed the way you'd actually look for them.

- **Shop and Jobs are separate destinations.** Both sit on the bottom bar, and
  each keeps its own place — switching between them returns you where you were
  instead of starting over.
- **Nine departments instead of "academic" and "student life".** That old split
  described the *seller*, not the product, which is how phones, hostels, hair
  and food all ended up filed under student life. There are now Electronics,
  Books & Study, Housing, Fashion & Beauty, Food, Services, Transport, Events
  and Campus Essentials.
- **Shop by department.** Tap **Departments** to walk down from a department to
  a category to the exact thing — Electronics → Phones & Tablets → Smartphones.
  Breadcrumbs sit above your results and widen the search a step at a time.
- **Shop opens on everything.** Browse starts on All rather than one
  department, so you never land on an empty shelf.
- **Filter by condition.** New, like new, good or fair — the app has always
  asked sellers for it and never let you search on it.
- **Selling is a drill-down too.** Choosing what you're listing used to be a
  wall of 104 chips; it's now the same three steps buyers take, so things get
  filed where buyers look. "Something else" works now — it used to do nothing
  at all — and asks you to name the type.
- **An empty subcategory offers the shelf above it** instead of asking you to
  create a listing.
- **Community, Groups and People are hidden for now** so Discover concentrates
  on the marketplace and jobs. Nothing was deleted.

**Fixes**

- The marketplace could tell you it was "in a private pilot" when the real
  problem was a dropped connection or a slow server — and because the check ran
  once, it stayed that way until you restarted the app. It now retries, says
  plainly when it can't reach us, and offers a Try again button.
- Publishing a study pack from a deck of 45 cards or more failed with no useful
  message. Deck-sized packs publish.
- Job links, job notifications and the Jobs shortcut inside the marketplace all
  stopped working when Jobs moved to its own tab. They work again.
