# Lantern Study 1.0.38

Chat: reactions, a Delete button that is actually there, and a selection you can see.

- **Emoji reactions.** Long-press any message and pick from 👍 ❤️ 😂 🎉 🙏 😮 😢 🔥
  — in group chats and DMs, on questions as well as ordinary messages. Tap a
  reaction chip to add or remove yours; everyone sees them live.
- **Delete is a real button now.** It used to hide inside the "More" menu,
  which disappears when it has nothing else to show — so on your own message
  it often vanished entirely. It now sits in the action bar. When a message
  cannot be deleted, the app says why instead of silently hiding the option.
- **Selecting a message highlights it.** The 1.0.37 highlight never actually
  drew anything; the long-pressed message is now clearly tinted.
- **Colours that were quietly missing are back.** A palette bug meant every
  faded tint, divider and progress-bar fill in the app rendered as nothing —
  including the "New messages" divider in chat and the mastery bars.
