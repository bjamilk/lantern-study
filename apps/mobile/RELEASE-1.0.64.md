# Lantern Study 1.0.64

The recording screen is built for a lecture hall, and your plan follows your
syllabus.

- **A recorder laid out for class.** Your notes sit in the middle, the
  transcript in its own drawer beside them, and one small tab strip
  underneath — **My Notes · Enhanced Notes · Material · Audio Files · Record**.
  Start recording and the drawer fills with what is being said, a level meter,
  the clock and a red stop. Need the whole screen for notes? Minimise the drawer
  to a small widget and bring it back with one tap. Stop, and it asks whether to
  turn the transcript into notes; the enhanced notes appear in your editor as
  they are written.
- **Your plan in your syllabus's order.** Upload a syllabus and the plan names
  and orders its units by the week their material belongs to, puts the exams
  between them as markers, and lists the weeks you have no material for yet
  under **Coming up in your syllabus** — so nothing in the plan opens onto
  nothing.
- **"Skip for now" sticks.** Skip the sync card on your phone and it stays
  skipped on the web, and the other way round.

**Under the floor:** on a shared browser the web upload tray could show another
student's upload titles; it now belongs to whoever is signed in.

**Not yet:** the recorder has not had a pass with a real classroom microphone,
so if the level meter or the quality reading looks wrong in a real room, tell
us — the recording and transcript underneath are unchanged from 1.0.63.
