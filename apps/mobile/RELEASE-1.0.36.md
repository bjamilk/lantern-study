# Lantern Study 1.0.36

Small fixes release.

- Choosing **"Don't show again"** on the feature tips now sticks permanently —
  on this device, on the web app, and across sign-ins. Previously the app
  could quietly undo that choice while syncing, bringing the tips back.
- **Community chat is now live**: every community's shared lounge (added in
  1.0.35) is switched on server-side — open your community in Discover and
  tap "Community chat" to say hi.
