# Lantern Study 1.0.26

A big release for buying, selling, finding work, and studying on your phone — the mobile app now matches everything that shipped on the web this week.

## Jobs

- **Find the right role faster.** Browse now has every filter the website has: all job types, minimum pay, paid/unpaid, remote or on-site, and sort by trending, newest, or closing soon. The minimum-pay filter and “closing soon” sort were impossible to reach on your phone before — now they’re one tap away.
- **Real employer tools.** Manage your company from your phone: upload a logo, edit your details, and add or remove team members. You can also read applicants’ screening answers right in the app.
- **A profile you fill once.** Add your headline, phone, and location alongside your résumé, and they go out with every application.
- **Honest listings.** Job posts can’t go live with the template placeholders still in them, and once a job’s deadline passes it stops taking applications.
- **No dead ends after an offer.** When an offer is accepted, “Pay now” and “View order” take you straight to it.

## Marketplace

- **What you see is what you can do.** Sold-out and expired listings no longer dangle a Buy button that only fails at checkout.
- **Sales that stick.** Editing a listing no longer quietly erases its sale end date, and you can filter by item condition.
- **Bundles work.** Creating a bundle now goes through instead of failing.

## Studying

- **Smarter flashcard review.** Every grade button now shows how far out it’ll schedule the card, you can undo your last grade, and finishing a session shows a clean summary of how you did.
- **Nothing slips through.** A card you’ve struggled with no longer disappears from your review queue, and turning a note into flashcards or a quiz actually saves them.
- **Cleaner notes.** Note previews read as plain text instead of raw formatting marks, and deleting a note asks first.
